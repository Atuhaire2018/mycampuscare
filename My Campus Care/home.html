MY CAMPUS CARE

Students Management Appointment System

Your university clinic appointment and counselling portal

Our Services

MyCampusCare lets students browse medical and counseling services, check staff availability, and book, reschedule or cancel appointments from their phone or computer, with automatic reminders. It also gives health workers and administrators tools to manage schedules and appointments, so students avoid long queues and wasted trips.

About Us

MyCampusCare is a campus app that lets students book medical and counseling appointments online and see staff availability before they go. It cuts queues and wasted trips, and helps staff manage schedules while keeping student health information private.
