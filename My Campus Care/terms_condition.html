The short version

  • MyCampusCare helps you book and manage appointments. It is not an emergency service.
  • Use your own account, keep your password private, and give accurate details.
  • Cancel or reschedule if you cannot attend so another student can take the slot.
  • Your health and counseling information is treated as sensitive and only shown to those who need it.
  • Please read the full terms below. Using the app means you accept them.
In an emergency, do not use this app. If you or someone else is in immediate danger or needs urgent medical help, go to the nearest health facility or call the emergency services right away. If you are in distress and cannot wait for an appointment, contact campus security or a trusted person near you straight away.

1About MyCampusCare

MyCampusCare is a mobile and web platform run for the University’s medical and counseling departments. It lets students view services, see staff working hours, book, cancel and reschedule appointments, and receive reminders.

The app handles scheduling and communication. It does not give medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Care is provided in person by qualified staff, and nothing on the platform replaces a consultation with them.

2Who can use it

MyCampusCare is for currently registered students of the University, and for the health workers, counselors and administrators the University has authorized. Staff and administrator accounts are created by the University and cannot be opened by the public.

By registering you confirm that you are a student of the University and that the details you give are true.

3Your account

  • Use your own name and details. Do not register or book on behalf of another person unless the University has allowed it.
  • Choose a strong password and keep it to yourself. You are responsible for activity that happens under your account.
  • Tell us quickly if you think someone else has accessed your account, so we can secure it.
  • Keep your email address and phone number up to date so reminders reach you.

4Booking appointments

You can book a medical or counseling appointment by choosing a service, a professional and an open time slot. A booking is confirmed once you receive a confirmation in the app or by message.

  • Slots are limited. The University sets how many students can be seen in each slot and may change this when needed.
  • Cancelling and rescheduling. If you cannot attend, cancel or reschedule as early as you can so the slot can be offered to someone else.
  • Missed appointments. Repeatedly missing appointments without notice may lead to temporary limits on how many bookings you can hold at once.
  • Changes by staff. A professional may become unavailable. If so, we will notify you and help you rebook as soon as possible.
  • Arrival. Please arrive a few minutes early. A late arrival may mean your slot is shortened or reassigned.

5Reminders and notifications

We may send appointment confirmations, reminders and notices of changes by push notification, email or SMS. Messages are kept discreet and show only what is needed, for example the time and the department. Standard message or data charges from your provider may apply.

Reminders are a courtesy. You remain responsible for knowing when your appointment is, even if a message does not arrive.

6Your information

To run the service we collect your student ID, name, programme, email and contact details, together with your appointment history. When you attend, staff may record medical or counseling information as part of your care.

Under Uganda’s Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019, health status and medical records are special personal data. We therefore:

  • collect only what is needed to deliver and improve the service;
  • use your information for scheduling, care, reminders and, in anonymized form, for planning and reporting;
  • limit access by role, so staff see only what their work requires;
  • protect data with secure login, encryption, audit logs and regular backups;
  • keep records only as long as the University’s retention rules and the law require;
  • never sell your information or use it for advertising.

You may ask to see the personal data we hold about you, to correct it, or to raise a concern. You may also complain to the Personal Data Protection Office (PDPO), Uganda’s data protection authority.

7Confidentiality and its limits

What you share with a doctor, nurse or counselor is treated as confidential. Appointment records are visible only to you and the staff involved in your care, and administrators see summaries without clinical detail.

There are a few situations where information may have to be shared:

  • when there is a serious risk of harm to you or to another person;
  • when a court or other legal authority lawfully requires it;
  • when you give your consent for it to be shared.

Where possible, your care provider will explain this to you first.

8Acceptable use

Please do not:

  • give false information or book appointments you do not intend to attend;
  • try to see another person’s records, or get around the app’s security;
  • harass, threaten or abuse staff or other users through the platform;
  • upload harmful files or interfere with how the system works;
  • use the platform for anything unlawful.

The University may suspend or restrict an account that breaks these rules.

9Staff and administrators

Staff and administrators must use MyCampusCare only for their official duties, keep their schedules accurate, and open only the records they need. Access is logged. Misuse of student information may lead to disciplinary action and, where the law requires, legal consequences.

10Availability of the service

We aim to keep MyCampusCare available at all times, but we cannot promise it will always be uninterrupted or error free. Maintenance, network problems or other issues may cause delays. If the app is down, please contact the medical or counseling department directly.

11Responsibility

The platform is provided to support access to campus health services. To the extent the law allows, the University and the developers are not responsible for losses caused by service interruptions, missed or delayed messages, or by information you entered incorrectly. Nothing in these terms limits any right you have under Ugandan law or the University’s duty to provide proper professional care.

12Changes and ending use

We may update these terms as the service grows or the law changes. When we make a significant change, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect. Continuing to use MyCampusCare afterwards means you accept the updated terms.

You can stop using the app and ask for your account to be closed at any time. Records that the University must keep for health or legal reasons will be retained as required.

13Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of Uganda, including the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019. We encourage you to raise any concern with us first so we can try to resolve it fairly and quickly.
