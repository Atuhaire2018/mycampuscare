The Student's Health and Management System is a project designed to improve the management of students' access to the clinical and counselling services within Uganda Christian University.

The Problem it solves

Right now , a student often has to walk either to the health centre or counselling office to find out whether the doctor, nurse or counselor they need is available .That means it could result into wasted time (incase the service provider is unavaiable), long queues, crowded when many students arrive at once , and less or no communication when appointments change. Some students ,especially those who may need counselling services ,may skip seeking help altogether because of this hassle

How it works

There are three kinds of users;

  • Students register and login , browse medical and counselling services, see which professionals are available and their working hours, pick an open slot , and get a confirmation and reminders. They can also cancel or reschedule
  • Medical and counseling staff get a dashboard where they set working hours, update availability, manage their daily appointments ,and mark them completed.
  • Administrators register staff, manage services and slot capacity, monitor appointments,and generate reports to help plan staffing

Privacy and security

Because the system handles health data, its designed around secure login, role-based access control, encryption, audit logs and backups. The document also ties it to Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019, which treats health records as special personal data.

Expected Benefits

The main benefits include; improving how students access the clinical and counselling services, resulting in less congestion and waiting time, better staff scheduling, clearer communication, and appointment data that administrators can use for planning. It would also support the university's transition towards digital systems.
